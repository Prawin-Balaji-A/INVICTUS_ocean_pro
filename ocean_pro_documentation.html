1. Executive Architecture Overview

Ocean Pro is an end-to-end, scientifically grounded 3D oceanographic simulation and autonomous marine ecosystem. It pairs high-performance WebGL graphics (physical water shaders, caustic projection, light attenuation, and volumetric atmosphere) with high-fidelity agent artificial intelligence and authentic global marine science data.

Key Engineering Paradigm
Ocean Pro separates instant local execution from background scientific enrichment. The client maintains an authoritative local Marine Knowledge Base (MarineKnowledgeBase.js) providing zero-latency biological profiles from frame 0, while asynchronous background research via the Gemini backend enriches and validates species metadata via live WoRMS and FishBase queries without hitching the 60 FPS WebGL render loop.

3. Feature Breakdown: "For What Feature We Used What"

3.1. Photorealistic Ocean Water & Underwater Optical Atmosphere

Feature Component Technologies & Modules Used Implementation Details
Wave Synthesis Custom GLSL Vertex Shader, Three.js ShaderMaterial Multi-octave Gerstner wave equations running on the GPU. Computes analytical wave steepness, wavelength, direction, and displacement vector with normal Jacobian calculation.
Water Surface Optics Custom GLSL Fragment Shader, Three.js PlaneGeometry Fresnel reflection, Cook-Torrance specular highlights, dynamic foam generation at crests based on Jacobian compression thresholds, and normal map perturbance.
Underwater Caustics Three.js TextureLoader, Additive Blending, GLSL Dynamic high-contrast caustic light maps projected downward onto the seabed and marine life bodies, fading with depth according to exponential light absorption.
Light Absorption & Turbidity Beer-Lambert Law formulation, Three.js FogExp2 Wavelength-dependent absorption: red light attenuates within 5m, yellow/green within 25m, leaving deep cyan/blue hues down to the aphotic seabed at -148m.
Volumetric God Rays Three.js Post-Processing, Custom Pass in Post.js Screen-space radial blur pass taking bright sun specular samples and projecting luminous volumetric shafts through the water column.

3.2. Camera Navigation & Vehicular Steering

Feature Component Technologies & Modules Used Implementation Details
Boat / Car-Style Steering HybridControls.js, Vector Math, Euler Angles A/D Keys: Perform continuous heading rotation without lateral strafing. W/S Keys: Propel along forward/backward vector. W+A / W+D: Produce curved arcs.
Vertical Propulsion HybridControls.js, Vertical Kinematics Q/E and Shift/Space allow smooth diving under the thermocline, exploring deep abyssal reefs, or resurfacing with water-transition detection.
Pointer Freedom DOM Mouse Event Listeners, Three.js Raycaster Pointer lock is intentionally avoided so the cursor is always available to click Codex cards, radar, advisory filters, and toolbar controls. Mouse drag orbits view.

3.3. Autonomous Marine Life & Multi-Agent Ecosystem AI

Feature Component Technologies & Modules Used Implementation Details
Model Ingestion & Centering Three.js GLTFLoader, SkeletonUtils Surgically offsets model bounding-box centers (pivotGroup) to eliminate Blender coordinate offsets, guaranteeing all creatures stay sub-surface ($Y \le -2.5\text{m}$).
Skeletal Animation Three.js AnimationMixer, Clip matching Detects swim clips from GLTF animations (findSwimClip), applying speed-synchronized cadence playback (timeScale) so tail frequencies match propulsion.
Real-Time Schooling SchoolManager.js, Steering.js Craig Reynolds Boids algorithm executing every frame at 60 FPS. Groups yellowback fusiliers, tangs, and discus with continuous flocking synchronization.
Predator-Prey Food Web TargetSelection.js, DecisionSystem.js Evaluates prey eligibility against predator taxonomy (huntsTaxa). Triggers predator CHASE and ATTACK lunges, while prey trigger evasive FLEE zig-zag bursts.
Depth & Habitat Clamping OceanEnvironment.js, EcosystemAI.js Strict biological depth clamping prevents creatures from breaching above $-2.5\text{m}$ or penetrating through the seabed floor at $-148\text{m}$.

5. Oceanographic Instrumentation & Scientific Simulations

5.1. Autonomous Instruments

  • Argo Profiling Floats (ArgoManager.js): Modeled after the International Argo Program. Floats drift neutrally at 1,000m, execute deep vertical profiling casts down to 2,000m, and ascend to the surface to transmit CTD (Conductivity, Temperature, Depth) telemetry.
  • Autonomous Underwater Gliders (GliderManager.js): Modeled after NOAA IOOS ocean gliders. Utilize internal mineral oil bladders for buoyancy-driven sawtooth flight profiles through coastal and pelagic transects without propellers.
  • Benthic Shipwrecks & Habitats (ShipwreckManager.js): Sunken galleon asset positioned on the abyssal seafloor (-146m) acting as an artificial reef congregating demersal species.

5.2. Scientific Simulation Modules

  • Potential Fishing Zone (PFZ) Advisory (PFZEngine.js): Simulates oceanic thermal gradients, SST front delineation, and chlorophyll concentration boundaries where pelagic fish congregate.
  • SAR (Search and Rescue) Leeway Drift Engine (DriftSimulationEngine.js): Monte Carlo drift dispersion calculation integrating surface current vectors, 10-meter windage vectors, and Stokes wave drift to project datum search areas.
  • Geostrophic Eddies (EddySimulation.js): Simulates cyclonic (cold core, upwelling) and anticyclonic (warm core, downwelling) ocean vortices.
